Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

Khushi Mail is a private tool used only by the owner of the Gmail accounts it connects to. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • Gmail messages, labels and filters in accounts whose owner signs in and approves access.

How the data is used

  • Only to read, search, label, archive and send mail on the account owner's own instructions.
  • Data stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking stops all access straight away.
